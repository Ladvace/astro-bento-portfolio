## Overview

[Allinmap](https://allinmap.app/) is an iOS and Android app for creating custom maps for travel, events and more, and for finding public amenities worldwide, like toilets, benches, water fountains and ATMs.

## The app

I developed the frontend of the app with React Native, Expo and TypeScript. Maps are rendered with Mapbox, and a lot of the work was handling GeoJSON data to draw places and custom layers on the map.

## The website

I also built the website with Astro and Mapbox, including a programmatic SEO setup that generates pages at scale to drive organic traffic to the app.
