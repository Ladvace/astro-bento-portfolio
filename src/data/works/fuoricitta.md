## Overview

From 2021 to 2022 I worked as a front-end developer at Fuoricittà, on projects for some of its larger clients.

## Projects

### BCC Relax Banking (Banca di Credito Cooperativo)

Relax Banking is the online banking platform of BCC, the Italian network of cooperative banks. I undertook a major migration of its front end, converting class-based React components to hooks and making the codebase easier to maintain. Alongside the migration, I fixed bugs and implemented new features across the platform.

### Maserati

A front-end system for managing Maserati's exhibition vehicles, with a custom multilingual interface and calendar features.

### AGI and Italian news sites

News websites for major Italian newspapers, built with Next.js (SSR) and TypeScript and integrated with a custom CMS. Among them, a brand-new site for [AGI](https://www.agi.it/), one of Italy's major news agencies, which significantly improved its scalability, performance and user experience.
