## Overview

From 2022 to 2023 I worked as a front-end developer at Letscodeit, building marketing sites and interactive campaigns.

## Projects

### Occupy Wall Street

I designed and developed a dynamic Next.js landing page for [occupywallstreet.com](https://occupywallstreet.com/) with advanced GSAP animations. It's integrated with Contentful CMS, so the team can change images, titles and text without touching code.

### Panda Express

A React-based scratch-off minigame for Panda Express's Chinese New Year campaign, where users could play to win prizes.
