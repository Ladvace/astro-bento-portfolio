## Overview

VirtualE is a workplace well-being app. I led the development of its Electron + React desktop app, which helps employees through mood tracking and other wellness features.

## Working with researchers

The features are backed by a research team of psychologists, so a big part of the work was turning their methods into an app employees would actually use day to day.
