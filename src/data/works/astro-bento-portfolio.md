## Overview

A bento-grid personal portfolio that doubles as an open-source template. It started in 2023 as a single-page grid of cards and has grown through several redesigns since.

## Highlights

- Switchable UI themes — glass, sharp, neon and paper styles, with color variants
- A guestbook where visitors leave messages and reactions, backed by libSQL
- An interactive 3D globe and a travel map of visited countries
- A playground of animation and interaction experiments
- Subtle, toggleable UI sound effects

## Making it a template

Everything personal lives in `src/site-config.ts`, and `pnpm site-setup` walks new users through filling it in.
