## Overview

groupcards.app is a group greeting card platform. I joined in 2025 as a full-stack developer and still work there today, mostly on its corporate product, where every company gets its own subdomain.

## What I built

- **Team management** — a system for companies to organise their teams, with a React + TypeScript frontend and an Express + TypeScript backend on MongoDB, deployed under the multi-tenant subdomain structure.
- **Corporate billing** — the billing system behind company subscriptions, a key feature for the business product, built on the same stack and tenant setup.

Alongside these, I keep improving the platform's stability and user experience across the full stack through new features, codebase improvements and bug fixes.
