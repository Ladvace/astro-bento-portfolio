## Overview

In 2022 I worked as a front-end developer at JAKALA, on a promotional platform.

## Micro frontend platform

I engineered a modular micro frontend platform with Qiankun and React, paired with a custom CMS for adjusting features.

## Minigames

Interactive minigames built with GSAP and Phaser.js. Users uploaded a receipt to play, and could win prizes. I developed the receipt-upload system too, which increased the number of promotional entries.
