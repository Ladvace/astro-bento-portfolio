## Overview

GDLauncher is a launcher for Minecraft that makes installing and managing game instances and mods simple. I co-founded it at GorillaDevs Inc, and it grew to thousands of users.

## My role

- Designed the interface in Figma and led front-end development, turning those designs into a Solid.js app on top of a Rust core.
- Planned product features and worked closely with the team to ship them.
- Managed hiring and supervised the frontend and design teams.
