This is a sample post on the demo site of [astro-bento-portfolio](https://github.com/Ladvace/astro-bento-portfolio). Fork the repo and replace it with your own writing.

## Text

Posts are plain Markdown, so you get **bold**, _italic_, `inline code` and [links](https://astro.build) for free.

> Blockquotes work too, for when someone else said it better.

## Lists

- Write posts in `src/data/blog/`
- Each file becomes a page at `/blog/<file-name>`
- Every post also lands in the RSS feed at `/rss.xml`

1. Write
2. Commit
3. Deploy
